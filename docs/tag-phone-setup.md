# Tag phone setup

During the demo your phone acts as the NFC tag on a classroom door.
Wong Kang taps a phone running the Attend app against yours.
Your phone runs the Attend Tag app and pretends to be one of three doors: LT2 Door A, LT2 Door B, or 2.506 Door.

## 1. What your phone needs

- An Android phone with NFC.
  iPhones cannot do this.
- Card emulation (HCE).
  Most Android phones with NFC have it.

You do not need to work this out yourself.
Install the app (step 2) and open it.
If your phone cannot do it, the app tells you on its first screen:

- "This phone has no NFC" means it cannot be the tag phone.
- "This phone has NFC but no card emulation (HCE)" means it cannot be the tag phone either.

In both cases, tell Wong Kang so someone else can do it.

## 2. Install the app

Wong Kang sends you a file named `app-release.apk`.

1. Open the file on your phone, from Files, Downloads, or the chat app it came in.
2. If your phone says installs from this source are not allowed, tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source", then go back.
3. Tap Install.
4. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap "More details" and then "Install anyway".
   The app is not in the Play Store, so this warning is expected.
5. If the install fails with "App not installed" and you had an older Attend Tag on the phone, uninstall the old one and try again.
   Tell Wong Kang if you do this, because it restarts the tag's counters.

The app appears as "Attend Tag".

## 3. Before the demo

1. Turn NFC on.
   Swipe down for quick settings and tap NFC, or search for "NFC" in Settings.
2. Open Attend Tag.
3. Check that it is ready: the top shows a door name, there is a list under "Act as door", and there is no yellow warning.
   If it says "NFC is off", turn NFC on and come back to the app.
4. Pick 2.506 Door, the first door in the demo.
5. Set the screen timeout to 10 minutes or longer, so the screen does not turn off during the demo.
6. Keep the phone unlocked with Attend Tag open on screen.
   The tag only works while the screen is on, and the app shows each tap only while it is open.
7. Do one practice tap with Wong Kang before the demo starts.

Your default payment or wallet app (Google Wallet, Samsung Wallet) does not get in the way.
Attend Tag is not registered as a payment app, so you do not need to change any payment setting.

If a tap ever shows a choice of apps, pick Attend Tag.
This only happens if another app on your phone claims the same kind of NFC tag.

## 4. During the demo

### How to hold the phones

1. Hold your phone screen up, so you can see the app.
2. Wong Kang brings the back of the Attend phone to the back of yours.
   Back to back works best.
3. On most phones the NFC antenna is in the middle of the back, or near the camera.
   Move the phones slowly around that area until the Attend phone reacts.
4. After each tap, pull the phones apart.
   Holding them together does not count as a new tap.

Each tap shows on your screen: "Reads served" goes up by one and "Last read" shows the time.

### Which door, and when

Switch doors only when Wong Kang asks.
Tap the door's name under "Act as door".
The new door applies from the next tap.

| Order | Door | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.506 Door | Wrong room, so the Attend app refuses the tap. |
| 2 | LT2 Door A | The tap in. |
| 3 | LT2 Door A | The tap out. Stay on LT2 Door A. |
| 4 (if asked) | LT2 Door B | A second door for the same class, to show it also works. |

Your door choice is remembered if the app closes.
Check the big door name at the top before each tap.

## 5. Do not touch

- **Replay last reading.**
  It makes the next tap repeat the previous one, which the Attend app refuses as a copied tap.
  Leave it off unless Wong Kang asks for it.
- **Reset counter.**
  Each tap carries a number that goes up by one.
  Reset puts it back to the start, so the Attend app sees numbers it has already used and refuses the next taps as copies.
- Do not clear the app's data or uninstall it before the demo, for the same reason.

If you press Reset counter by mistake, tap Cancel.
If you already confirmed it, tell Wong Kang.

## 6. If something goes wrong

**Nothing happens when the phones touch.**

1. Check the screen is on, unlocked, and showing Attend Tag.
2. Check there is no yellow "NFC is off" warning.
3. Pull the phones apart, then try again, moving slowly over the middle and the camera area of the back.
4. Remove thick phone cases or metal cases.
5. Close Attend Tag fully and open it again.

**The Attend app says wrong room or wrong door.**
Check the door name at the top of your screen and pick the one Wong Kang asked for.
Then tap again.

**The app says no NFC or no card emulation (HCE).**
Your phone cannot be the tag phone.
Tell Wong Kang straight away.
